# Body selector visual assets

## Provenance

The front and rear body-selector figures were created specifically for Health Check Lab on 2026-09-22 with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool. They are not copied from a stock library, medical atlas, or third-party website.

- `body-selector-front-480.webp` and `body-selector-front-768.webp`: responsive exports of the generated front view.
- `body-selector-back-480.webp` and `body-selector-back-768.webp`: responsive exports of the matching generated rear view.
- The rear view was generated from the front view as a composition and style reference. It is a true rear view, not a horizontally mirrored front image.
- Source output: 1024 x 1536 RGBA PNG per view.
- Published output: 480 x 720 and 768 x 1152 WebP with alpha transparency, quality 88.

## Intended use

These assets are project-specific interface imagery for the Health Check Lab body-part selector. Their use remains subject to the OpenAI terms that apply to the account which generated them. No external attribution, hotlink, or third-party asset license is required by this repository.

The images intentionally contain no text, markers, diagnoses, pain effects, or highlighted body regions. Labels, selection states, links, and region highlights are rendered as HTML and CSS so they remain accessible and responsive.

## Generation direction

The shared direction was a fully covered, gender-neutral adult healthcare avatar with natural proportions, a neutral stance, a matte white surface, subtle light-gray depth, transparent background, and matching front/rear framing. The prompts explicitly excluded realistic skin, detailed facial features, exposed anatomy, organs, visible musculature, wounds, logos, and embedded text.
